Common questions

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. We start with the basics and build confidence with good form before adding load.

I’m busy. How often do I need to train?

Tell me which days you can realistically train and I’ll plan around that, rather than the other way round.

I have aches or an old injury. Can you help?

I choose exercises that work around everyday niggles. I’m not a doctor or physio, so for ongoing pain or an injury, please get medical advice first.
